# AI Opportunity Assessment

Turn a pile of possible AI use cases into a ranked, defensible business case.

## Problem

Companies have dozens of possible AI use cases but no transparent, repeatable way to prioritize them.
Prioritization is usually done in ad-hoc spreadsheets with hidden assumptions.
This tool generates use cases from business processes, scores them (value, feasibility, risk incl. EU AI Act) and builds a business case.

## Limitations

Gains are valued as freed time, not cash savings: they become cash only if capacity is reduced or redeployed.
LLM-prefilled criteria tend to cluster (optimism and central-tendency bias), which is why the matrix defaults to thresholds relative to the portfolio rather than a fixed 50/50 bar (see docs/decisions.md).
